# Aliit Fellowship -- Cohort 2 Application

## Contribution Persona: Builder + Educator

## Who I Am

I am a blockchain developer working from Japan, focused entirely on building production infrastructure for the Midnight network. I operate under the principle that a privacy-first ecosystem only works if its tools are equally first-class. I am the creator and sole developer of NightForge Explorer, the most comprehensive Midnight block explorer in existence, and I am applying to the Aliit Fellowship as both a Builder and Educator.

## What I Have Built

NightForge Explorer (nightforge.jp) is a full-featured, open-source blockchain explorer serving all three Midnight networks: mainnet, preprod, and preview. It runs in production today, deployed on dedicated infrastructure with systemd auto-restart and nginx routing, available to every Midnight user worldwide.

Here is what NightForge delivers that no other Midnight tool currently provides:

**Privacy Analytics.** A 99% shielded ratio dashboard derived from on-chain event analysis, giving the community its first real-time visibility into how effectively Midnight's privacy guarantees are being used. ZK verified badges appear on shielded transactions, making the privacy layer tangible and legible.

**Governance Tracking.** Full on-chain governance monitoring, including council proposals, voting records, and technical committee activity -- the kind of transparency a decentralized network needs to hold itself accountable.

**Cross-Chain Visibility.** Cardano anchor visualization that maps the relationship between Midnight and its settlement layer, plus a bridge monitor that has tracked over 36,000 bridge operations to date.

**Network Intelligence.** An epoch timeline with countdown, network health scoring, a 10-validator committee display, block producer tracking via the official indexer, event type breakdowns, and a live block pulse animation that makes network liveness immediately visible.

**Contract Ecosystem.** Tracking of 19 deployed contracts and over 1,200 contract calls, with an interactive API documentation layer that serves as a developer on-ramp.

**Localization.** A Japanese/English language toggle covering 114 translated interface elements -- the first Midnight tool to serve the Japanese market natively.

**Education.** A built-in NIGHT token and DUST fee mechanism explainer, a network age counter, and clear visualizations designed to make Midnight understandable to newcomers.

The entire project is open source at github.com/cryptoleo79/NIGHTFORGE.

## Why This Matters

Midnight is a new network. Its success depends on infrastructure that makes the protocol legible, trustworthy, and accessible. NightForge is the first explorer to treat privacy metrics as a primary feature rather than an afterthought. It is also the first Midnight tool built for the Japanese market, one of the most active blockchain communities in the world but one that is currently underserved by Midnight's ecosystem.

## What I Will Build Next

My roadmap includes an SPO/validator directory, DUST fee visualization dashboards, deeper GraphQL indexer integration for richer query support, and expansion to additional languages beyond Japanese and English. Each of these extends Midnight's reach to new users and developers.

## How I Will Contribute as a Fellow

As a Builder, I will continue shipping production infrastructure and contributing open-source tooling. As an Educator, I will produce technical content, tutorials, and developer guides -- in both English and Japanese -- that lower the barrier to building on Midnight. I will mentor developers in Asia, organize community engagement in Japan, and represent Midnight at regional events. The Aliit Fellowship is not a starting point for me; it is recognition of work already done and a commitment to do more.
